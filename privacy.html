Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 10, 2025 | Version 2.1.0

Our Commitment to Privacy

Social Filter is designed with privacy-first principles. We believe your browsing history is yours alone. This extension operates 100% locally on your device with absolutely zero data collection or transmission to external servers.

Data We Collect (Locally Only)

The following data is stored only on your local device using Chrome's storage API:

  • Social Media Profile Visits: Platform name, username, full URL, page title, and timestamp
  • User Preferences: Tracking settings, enabled platforms, max entries/days settings
  • Optional Notes: Any notes you manually add to tracked entries

Important: This data is stored in chrome.storage.local which means:

  • It stays on YOUR computer only
  • It is NOT synced to your Google account
  • It is NOT accessible to us (the developers)
  • It is NOT sent to any servers
  • Other extensions cannot access it

Data We DO NOT Collect

  • Your personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Your complete browsing history
  • Passwords or login credentials
  • Financial information
  • Any data from websites other than social media profiles
  • Analytics or usage statistics
  • Device information or identifiers

Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

Social Filter contains non-intrusive affiliate advertisements. These ads are displayed in the extension popup and are intended to support the development and maintenance of this free tool.

How Ads Work:

  • Dynamic Loading: Ads are loaded from a GitHub repository and may change periodically
  • Affiliate Links: Ads may contain affiliate links to e-commerce sites (Daraz, Amazon, etc.)
  • No Tracking: Ads do NOT track your browsing behavior or collect personal data
  • Non-Intrusive: Ads are confined to a small banner in the popup and do not interfere with extension functionality
  • Commission: If you click an ad and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you

Your Privacy with Ads:

  • Ads are NOT personalized based on your data
  • We do NOT share your data with advertisers
  • Ads do NOT use cookies or tracking pixels
  • Clicking ads opens the advertiser's website in a new tab - their privacy policy applies

Why Ads? Ads help keep Social Filter free while supporting ongoing development, bug fixes, and new features. We've designed them to be as unobtrusive as possible while maintaining full privacy protection.

Permissions Explained

Social Filter requests the following Chrome permissions:

1. History Permission

Why: To detect when you visit social media profile pages
What we access: Only URLs and page titles from social media sites
What we DON'T access: Your full browsing history or non-social media sites

2. Storage Permission

Why: To save tracked profiles locally on your device
What we store: Profile visit records and your preferences
Where: Only in chrome.storage.local (device-only storage)

3. Tabs Permission

Why: To open profile links when you click them in the extension
What we access: Only the ability to create new tabs
What we DON'T access: Tab content, passwords, or form data

4. Alarms Permission

Why: To periodically clean up old entries based on your settings
What we do: Delete entries older than your configured max days
Frequency: Once per hour

5. Host Permission (raw.githubusercontent.com)

Why: To load dynamic advertisements from our GitHub repository
What we access: Only ad content (text/images) from our public GitHub repo
Privacy: This request does NOT send any of your data to GitHub

What We Track (Locally)

Social Filter only tracks visits to these types of pages:

  • Instagram profiles (instagram.com/username)
  • TikTok profiles (tiktok.com/@username)
  • Facebook profiles (facebook.com/username)
  • X/Twitter profiles (x.com/username)
  • YouTube channels (youtube.com/@channel)
  • Reddit users (reddit.com/user/username)
  • LinkedIn profiles (linkedin.com/in/username)
  • Snapchat profiles (snapchat.com/add/username)

We do NOT track:

  • Individual posts, stories, or reels
  • Feed pages or explore pages
  • Search results
  • Messages or direct messages
  • Any other website

Data Security

  • All data is stored locally using Chrome's secure storage API
  • Data is isolated to your browser and cannot be accessed by websites
  • No data leaves your device unless YOU export it manually
  • Extension code is open-source and can be inspected

Your Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Pause Tracking: Toggle tracking on/off anytime from the popup
  • Platform Control: Enable/disable specific platforms in Settings
  • Delete Entries: Delete individual entries or all data from the dashboard
  • Export Data: Download your data as JSON or CSV anytime
  • Uninstall: Removing the extension deletes all stored data permanently
  • Max Entries/Days: Set automatic cleanup rules in Settings

Third-Party Services

GitHub (For Ads Only)

We fetch ad content from raw.githubusercontent.com. This request:

  • Does NOT send any of your personal data
  • Only downloads ad text/images
  • Is a simple HTTP GET request for a public JSON file
  • GitHub may log the request IP (standard web behavior) but we have no access to these logs

Affiliate Partners (When You Click Ads)

If you click on an affiliate ad and visit an external website (Daraz, Amazon, etc.):

  • You leave the extension environment
  • The external website's privacy policy applies
  • We do NOT control or access data collected by these sites
  • Your visit may be tracked by the affiliate partner for commission purposes

Children's Privacy

Social Filter is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you are under 13, please do not use this extension.
